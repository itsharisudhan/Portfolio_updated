About Me

Creative Eye.
Technical Mind.
Always Learning.

I'm Hari Hara Sudhan, a final year B.Tech student who found his true calling behind the timeline. While my academic background is in engineering, my heart is in video editing and visual storytelling. I see every project as a chance to blend technical precision with creative flair.

I started with mobile editing in VN and CapCut, quickly realizing that I wanted more control. This led me to Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects, where I now spend most of my creative energy. I'm passionate about videography and cinematography, always looking for the perfect shot to complement the edit.

Beyond editing, I'm a frontend enthusiast. I build web projects using React and JS, and I'm currently exploring Next.js and Supabase. I'm looking for a video editing internship where I can contribute my unique perspective, learn from the best, and grow into a professional creator.
